HOME NETWORK MONITOR  (Windows)

1. Unzip this folder (right-click the zip, Extract All).
2. Double-click HomeNetworkMonitor.exe.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - If Windows Firewall asks, click "Allow access".
3. Your web browser opens the dashboard. Create an account, then press "Scan now".

Keep the black window open while you use it. Close it to stop the program.
Your computer must be connected to the home Wi-Fi or cable to find your devices.
