HOME NETWORK MONITOR  (Mac)

1. Unzip this folder (double-click the zip).
2. FIRST TIME ONLY: right-click (or Control-click) the file "HomeNetworkMonitor" and choose Open,
   then click Open again in the warning. (macOS warns about apps that are not from the App Store.)
3. A Terminal window opens and your web browser shows the dashboard.
   Create an account, then press "Scan now".

Keep the Terminal window open while you use it. Close it to stop the program.
Your Mac must be connected to the home Wi-Fi or cable to find your devices.
